## Detailed Description (16,000 chars max)

Transform any web article into natural, high-quality audio with just one click! Article to Podcast uses advanced AI text-to-speech to convert blog posts, news articles, research papers, and more into audio you can listen to anywhere.

**🎧 Perfect for:**
• Commuting and driving
• Working out or running  
• Doing chores around the house
• Accessibility and reading assistance
• Multitasking professionals
• Visual rest and eye strain relief

**✨ Key Features:**

**🚀 One-Click Conversion**
Simply click our extension icon on any article page and we'll automatically extract the main content and convert it to crystal-clear audio using OpenAI's advanced text-to-speech technology.

**🎛️ Smart Playback Controls**
• Floating audio player that stays with you
• Variable speed control (0.75x to 2.0x)
• Progress tracking and skip controls
• Continue where you left off
• Download as MP3 for offline listening

**📱 Clean, Intuitive Interface**
• Minimal popup design that doesn't get in the way
• Real-time conversion progress with time estimates
• Smart content extraction (skips ads, navigation, comments)
• Works on any website with articles

**💰 Generous Free Tier**
• 10 full article conversions per month at no cost
• No account required to get started
• Transparent usage tracking

**🔒 Privacy-First Design**
• No data stored on our servers
• Article text processed securely through OpenAI
• No tracking or analytics of your reading habits
• Audio files stored locally on your device

**⚡ Lightning Fast**
Most articles convert in under 30 seconds. Our optimized pipeline ensures you're listening to your content quickly without long waits.

**🌐 Universal Compatibility**
Works on virtually any website with articles:
• News sites (CNN, BBC, The New York Times, etc.)
• Blogs and personal websites  
• Documentation and technical articles
• Research papers and academic content
• Medium, Substack, and other publishing platforms

**🎯 Why Choose Article to Podcast?**

Unlike expensive alternatives like Speechify ($139/year) or NaturalReader ($99/year), we offer professional-grade text-to-speech at an affordable price point. Our focus is on simplicity and effectiveness - no bloated features you'll never use.

**Coming Soon:**

**🛠️ Technical Excellence**
Built with modern Chrome Extension Manifest V3 standards for security, performance, and compatibility. Uses Mozilla's battle-tested Readability.js for accurate content extraction and OpenAI's state-of-the-art text-to-speech models for natural-sounding audio.

**💬 Perfect for Accessibility**
Whether you have dyslexia, visual impairments, or simply prefer audio learning, Article to Podcast makes web content accessible in a whole new way.

**🎓 Great for Learning**
Research shows that some people learn better through audio. Convert educational articles, documentation, and research papers to audio format for enhanced comprehension and retention.
